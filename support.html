Team Photo Planner support

Team Photo Planner is a row-planning tool planned for a free App Store release by Matthew “The Body” Kemmetmueller.

Get help

Contact: Me@matthewthebody.com. Include the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include player names or other sensitive information. Share a screenshot only if it contains no private details you wish to keep confidential.

Saved setups

Saved setups stay on your device. Reopen them from Saved setups. Deleting local data in Settings removes saved setups and resets the privacy choice; it cannot retract files or text you have already shared. Keep a separate copy of any formation you need to retain before deleting app data.

Formations

Simple mode uses one row for 1–4 people, the original three-row layout for 5, two rows for 6–7, and three-row planning for larger groups. Custom mode provides 3–12 rows. If a combination is infeasible, choose one of the offered alternatives without changing the head count. Check every face and adapt poses and spacing to the group.

Sharing

Share text or export a PNG using the device's share interface. Choose a destination to send, or dismiss the sheet to cancel. Opening the sheet alone does not confirm delivery. Destination apps may need internet access.

Privacy

No connected analytics, advertising SDK or developer cloud account is included in this version. Read the accompanying privacy policy for local storage, exports, external links and your controls.
