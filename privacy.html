Team Photo Planner — privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026. Published by Matthew “The Body” Kemmetmueller. This policy describes the current app with analytics disconnected.

Information on your device

Team Photo Planner stores saved setup names, head counts, row arrangements and your privacy preference locally on your device. The app does not send that information to the developer or operate a cloud setup account. Device backups may be managed separately by your operating system and backup settings.

Analytics and advertising

This version has no connected analytics service, advertising SDK, advertising identifier collection or location collection. Its analytics setting controls only a temporary in-memory policy buffer; no usage events are transmitted to the developer. Any future connected analytics requires an updated policy and appropriate controls before activation.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to share text or a PNG, the app prepares the content locally and opens your device's sharing interface. The service or recipient you choose receives the content you send and handles it under its own practices. Setup names can be included in shared text. An opened share sheet does not mean delivery occurred. Temporary export files can remain in the app's cache and may be removed by the operating system.

External links and support

Opening the community link takes you to a third-party service with its own privacy practices. If you contact support, the support operator receives the information you voluntarily provide. Public support contact: Me@matthewthebody.com. Do not send sensitive player information when requesting help.

Your controls

You can delete individual saved setups or use Settings > Delete local data to remove saved setups and reset the app's privacy choice. This does not retract content already shared with another service or recipient, or remove separately managed backups.

Apple services

App Store, TestFlight and operating-system services may process information independently under Apple's terms and privacy settings. This policy does not describe Apple as collecting no information.

Changes and contact

For privacy questions, contact Matthew Kemmetmueller at Me@matthewthebody.com. We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change.

## This support website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking pixels to these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement: GitHub Privacy Statement. This website hosting is separate from the app’s local calculation and storage.
